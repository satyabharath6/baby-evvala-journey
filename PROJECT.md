# 👶 Baby Evvala Journey

**Version:** 2.0

**Status:** Active Development

---

# Vision

Baby Evvala Journey is not just a gender reveal website.

It is a digital family keepsake that preserves the complete journey from pregnancy to birth, allowing family members to participate and creating memories that Baby Evvala can revisit years later.

Guiding Principle:

> Technology should stay invisible. Emotion should stay visible.

---

# Project Goals

- Preserve pregnancy memories
- Connect family across the world
- Create an unforgettable gender reveal
- Build a digital time capsule
- Continue growing after birth
